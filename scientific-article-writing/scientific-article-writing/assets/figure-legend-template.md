# Figure legend template

```text
Figure N. [Title sentence: the finding the figure shows, or a precise noun phrase naming its content.]
(A) [What is plotted: measure on y, factor on x, conditions with the same labels as the text. What the reader should see.]
(B) [Next panel.]
[Statistical notation: error bars or shading represent SEM / 95% CI; n = ...; test and correction; *p < 0.05, **p < 0.01, ***p < 0.001.]
[Cross-references: See also Figure SN and Methods, "Subsection name".]
```

Rules:

- The title sentence makes the same claim as the Results subsection header, at the same strength.
- Every abbreviation in the figure is defined in the legend or is field-universal.
- Condition labels in panels, legend, and text are identical.
- A legend describes what is shown; it never reports a result that is not in the figure or adds interpretation absent from Results.
- If individual data points are plotted, say so and say what each represents.
